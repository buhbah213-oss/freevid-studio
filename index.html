IMAGE → CINEMATIC CLIP

1. Add your reference image

Choose an image to preview your cinematic animation.
The prompt is saved with your settings, but this starter version animates the image using camera motion; it does not synthesize new AI frames.

2. Animation settings

Choose an image to begin.

Exports a WebM video supported by Android and most modern players. MP4 export depends on browser/encoder support.

3. Generate audio

Ambient audio is synthesized locally in your browser.

WAV export creates procedural ambience/effects. Spoken voice uses your Android text-to-speech engine and is played aloud; voice export to a file is not supported by every browser.

4. Save & install

Your image stays in this browser session. Exported files are downloaded to your phone. Add this app to your home screen from your browser menu to use it like an app.

Privacy: image processing and sound synthesis happen locally in this page. This app does not upload your image or call a paid API.
